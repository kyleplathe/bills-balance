# Privacy Policy

**Bills & Balance**  
Last updated: September 6, 2026

Bills & Balance is a local-first checkbook and bills app for iPhone and iPad.

## Your data stays on this device

Accounts, bills, income, and transactions are stored on your device using Core Data. There is no account to create. We do not operate a server that receives your ledger.

## Backup

Export Backup writes a file you choose to save in Files, iCloud Drive, or another app. Nothing is uploaded unless you share that file.

## Lock

Optional Face ID or device passcode lock keeps the app closed until you unlock it. That preference stays on this device. We never see your biometrics.

## Notifications

Bill reminders use Apple’s notification system on this device. Permission is optional.

## Bitcoin prices

If you add a digital wallet set to Bitcoin, the app may fetch public market prices from [CoinGecko](https://www.coingecko.com/). That request does not include your balances, bills, or identity. Bitcoin features stay hidden until you opt in.

## What we do not collect

We do not collect contact info, financial info, location, or analytics for our own servers. We do not sell data. We do not use tracking.
